# Session recap, 21 Oct 2026

**Previously on...**

The Partnership of the Jade Frog arrived at Candlekeep from Baldur's Gate disguised as travelling performers. This was plausible, since they do have a real one with them. Thia called the act "somewhat successful." Llyr corrected her: "very unsuccessful." The dwarf servant stayed cheerfully oblivious. An official of Baldur's Gate's ruling class saw straight through them and offered a deal. If they find Massatar, the missing dwarf who runs the keep and keeps the Sanguine Vessel, they get an audience with Garstang. So down they went: a tower, Netherese-weave runes, a corkscrew ramp around a chasm, and a chest that produced a zombie with a crossbow in its mouth.

Past a gore-caked surgery was a mess room with a bloated zombie matron and a knock-kneed boy holding a platter. Thia suggested asking them about Massatar. She was "very thoroughly overruled." Sykth hexed the boy and put 55 damage through his chest. Thia's 4th-level Fireball hit the matron in the belly, and four head-spiders dropped from under her dress. Every wound produced more. Her Motherly Love gave her a free swing whenever one died nearby, so when Thia's Shatter killed six children, Llyr's fire elemental lost 37 HP in one go. The burning boy rose again as an eight-foot floating horror with a fanged chest-maw and multiplying heads ("a zombie teenager," per the DM). Then something skeletal in runic diver's armour shuffled in. Sykth tripwired it flat, and Llyr rolled two natural 20s for 42.

Thia's next spell finished the crabs and the matron. The kills took her fused wand to 12 points, which earns her a permanent 3rd-level slot (available immediately, by the same rule as the HP loss) and costs her permanent HP. The armoured "doorman" turned out to be an experimental magical slot machine that casts a random 3rd-level spell each turn. It cast Slow, then Enemies Abound, and Llyr hit his own fire elemental twice. Sykth Misty Stepped out of "Steam Room 2.0." Thia Thunderwaved the teen off the ledge. From the chasm came a wet thud, then two screams: one from the teen's five heads in sequence, one very low. Her acid Chromatic Orb (25 to hit, 24 damage) ate through the doorman's armour, and it collapsed.

Everyone got one action before bed. The statue turned out to be **Shar**, the goddess their lich employer Metheran worships. The obelisks flanking the stone door electrify anything passing between them. And Llyr found a hidden switch behind the statue and flipped it. Something to their right ground open, and the inhuman screaming started.
