# Session recap, 28 Oct 2026

**Previously on...**

The night opened with screaming behind a secret door, so Llyr, wild-shaped into a water elemental, sent his conjured fire elemental in first. Deep beneath occupied Candlekeep, the Partnership of the Jade Frog is still hunting the Sanguine Vessel, a lich's phylactery entrusted to Massatar, the dwarven necromancer of the Pale Circle. The elemental took 10 damage from the shock pillars on the way in, then was screamed to death by a ghoul with a loudspeaker for a chest. Llyr slammed the ghoul for 24, then 29, and burst it against the wall like a watermelon. Thia spotted a chained Death's Herald at the bottom of the shaft, still brawling with five-headed zombie boy. Sykth raided the galley and came back with orcish rum and two bottles of black wine, from a chest with a gap where a third might have been.

Massatar's study held a flattering self-portrait and a lot of reading. Llyr's natural 20 on the papers turned up an unsent letter. Massatar has gone after the Hollow Saint, an avatar of Shar no longer under Shar's control, last seen among the strays by Baldur's Gate's walls. Lord Garstang supplies him with "stock," and a colleague named Dera overcharged him for black wine. Llyr suspects the Saint is the little girl they saw outside. Thia, meanwhile, ritual-cast *Identify* on a chest to check it for danger. *Identify* requires touching the chest. She was teleported into a cell with a dung-covered floor. Sykth opened the chest with a cloth instead. There was no Vessel inside, but there were two black pearls, an obsidian dagger bearing Shar's sigil, a book called *Necrotic Nights*, two scrolls, and gems worth 200, 250 and 350 gp. Then he and Llyr touched the chest on purpose.

Downstairs, Thia had spent her Blink flickering in and out beside a bored, six-armed, key-wielding devil, purely to confuse it. She persuaded it to look in the cell. It looked, told Sykth and Llyr to behave themselves, and shut the hatch. Sykth stabbed it in the eye through that hatch for 24, and Llyr Stone Shaped the door into pebbles anyway. Moonbeam (18), a sculpted Fireball (13) and a natural-20 Shillelagh (24) followed. When the devil sealed Thia's lips with a silver key, she dug out Hypnotic Pattern, which has no verbal component, and charmed it. Offered a parley, the party declined. Sykth stabbed it with advantage for 30, which woke it up. It turned a smoke key on Llyr and conjured thousands of tiny closing doors. Llyr made the save, sparing himself an extradimensional hell, and took a fistful of keys for 9 instead. Then he lost Moonbeam. The devil is no longer charmed, nowhere near dead, and standing in a room full of doors. **Llyr acts next, then Thia.**
